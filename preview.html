Sandbox Guide

This is a **zero-dependency interactive mockup engine** for the Pet Sales App. It runs natively in your browser with no compiler overhead or CDN requirements.

  • Use the **Left Directory** to instantly toggle any of the 50 screens.
  • To test **Phone Verification**: click Sign In with Phone on Screen 5 or Register on Screen 6, type any phone number, and click proceed. A mock SMS notification banner will show the OTP.
  • Creating a pet listing adds details to lists in real time.
